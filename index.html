Accessibility
Game

Keyboard: type letters to spell · Enter submit · Backspace remove · S shuffle · H hint · U undo · P/Esc pause · arrows + Space to pick letters with focus.

Gamepad: dpad/stick move focus · A pick letter · B remove · X shuffle · Y hint · RB submit · Start pause.

How to play

Connect letters

Drag across the wheel — or type letters — to spell words of 3+ letters. Each letter can be used once per word.

Fill the grid

Words that belong to the crossword grid plant themselves and grow a flower. Find them all to complete the grove.

Bonus words

Real words that aren’t on the grid still score — and grow wildflowers.

Shuffle & hints

Shuffle only rearranges the wheel’s look. Hints reveal one grid letter for a small point cost.

Scoring

Grid words score by length. Using every letter in one word earns a full-wheel bonus. Results always show the breakdown.

Fair play

Seeds are fixed and inspectable. Ranked boards reject changed settings — challenge yourself, not the ledger.

Practice

Limited graphics

WebGL isn’t available, so the 3D grove is resting. The game is fully playable — your progress is safe.
